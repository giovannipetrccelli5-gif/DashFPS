## Join the DashSMP community 🎮

Looking for a survival multiplayer server with PvP and plenty of things to work toward? **DashSMP** brings Java and Bedrock players together through a shared Minecraft server.

Explore a custom survival economy, buy and sell items, trade with other players, play PvP, and progress through server-made systems.

### What you'll find

- **Java + Bedrock crossplay** through Geyser/Floodgate.
- **Crystal PvP** with a dedicated Crystal PvP shop category, plus other PvP gear.
- **Player economy** featuring `/shop`, `/sell`, `/ah` auction listings, and `/orders`.
- **Crates, shards, kits, vaults, and ranks** to explore.
- **Player teams, bounties, homes, and leaderboards**.
- **Custom tools and events**, including Amethyst and Shard items plus seasonal content.

Features and supported client versions can change; check the community Discord for current connection information and server updates.

## How to connect

1. Open Minecraft Java Edition or Bedrock Edition on a supported version.
2. Choose multiplayer / add server.
3. Enter **`dashsmp.xyz`** as the server address.
4. Join the [DashSMP Discord](https://discord.gg/WYSs5KEazR) for announcements, help, and to meet other players.
